# Magenta Melody asset not redistributed

The private hardware-validation candidate used `magenta_melody.wav` prepared
from user-provided audio. Public redistribution rights are not assumed, so the
WAV is intentionally omitted.

The audio runtime remains present. A private build may provide a compatible
PCM WAV at `runtime/TITAN/audio/magenta_melody.wav`. The system tolerates the
asset being absent.
